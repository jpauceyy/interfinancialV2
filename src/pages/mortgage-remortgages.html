Variable or fixed?

Understanding how your rate behaves is the first step to choosing a deal that suits your plans.

Standard variable
rate (SVR)

Moves with the market
  • The lender's standard rate, usually 2–4% above the Bank of England base rate
  • Variable-rate borrowers can switch to another lender without a penalty

Fixed rate
mortgage

Up to 25 years
  • Repayments stay the same for an agreed period, whatever happens to the base rate
  • When the fixed term ends, your mortgage reverts to the lender's SVR

Buying property abroad

Legal systems and buying processes vary from country to country, so take specialist mortgage advice before you commit.

  1. Choose your currency

    Where possible, take your mortgage in the currency you earn. If you'll receive rental income in the local currency, a local-currency mortgage may suit you better.

  2. Release equity at home

    Remortgaging your UK property may help you raise the deposit for a home overseas.

  3. Use local professionals

    Appoint independent solicitors, architects and surveyors who understand the local law and buying process.

  4. Get an independent valuation

    A valuation can reveal problems with the property and boundary disputes before you buy.

  5. Check before you sign

    Never sign anything you don't understand. Have the original contract, and any translation, checked by a specialist.
