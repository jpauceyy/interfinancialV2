Second charge loans

Secured loans in Market Drayton, Shrewsbury and Telford

Independent advice

A secured loan, also called a second charge loan, is a second mortgage on your residential or buy-to-let property. It ranks behind your main mortgage and lets you borrow against the equity you hold in your home.

How it works →

A second charge on your property

Your main mortgage stays exactly as it is. The secured loan sits behind it as a second charge, borrowing against the equity you've built up.

4.5% Typical starting rate per annum
Minimal Penalty tie-in periods

Reasons to
consider a secured loan

A second charge loan can help when a remortgage or high street lender isn't the right fit. It may suit you if:

  1. You want to keep preferential terms on your existing mortgage, such as a very low lifetime tracker rate

  2. You have impaired credit, a failed credit score, or an IVA or debt management plan

  3. You're an older borrower finding it hard to get credit

  4. Your financial affairs are complex

  5. You're self-employed, have a short employment history or recently changed jobs

  6. You need a higher loan-to-value than is otherwise available

  7. You've recently started trading as a limited company, partnership or sole trader

  8. You need funds for a purpose other lenders don't usually permit

No strict rules on how you use it

There are no set criteria for what a second charge loan can be used for, and that flexibility is one of its biggest advantages.

  • Debt consolidation
  • Tax bills
  • Home improvements
  • Business purposes
  • Buy-to-let purchase or deposit
  • Weddings
  • School fees
  • Helping children & family
